Privacy notice

AutoCom (“we”) collects the information you choose to send through this website’s enquiry and quotation forms: your name, phone number, optional email address, the products you ask about and your message.

Why we use it

We use it only to respond to your enquiry or quotation request and to keep a record of our conversation with you. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to serve you and for our business records, after which they are deleted.

Who can see it

Only authorised AutoCom staff can view enquiries, through a password-protected administration area.

Cookies

This website uses a single essential session cookie so forms and your quotation list work. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information you sent us through our contact page.
